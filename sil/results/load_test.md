## Before and after the fix (same test, same data)

| Cars | GET /hazards, old code | GET /hazards, new code |
|---|---|---|
| 25 | 1,404 ms | 25 ms |
| 50 | 5,413 ms | 20 ms |
| 100 | 16,143 ms | 21 ms |
| 200 | 52,840 ms | 72 ms |

What was wrong: every hazard query reloaded all positions and reports and re-ran the whole trust engine, and the
engine compared every hazard with every car's full position history. Fixes (decisions unchanged; proven by
`engine/tests/test_scaling.py` and identical system-simulation results):
1. the backend reuses the trust engine's decisions for up to 1 s when many drivers ask at once
   (`backend/icvsp_backend/service.py`);
2. the engine looks only at cars, reports and road segments in the 100 m grid cells around each hazard; anything
   that can reach further (poor GPS, gaps between positions) is still compared with everything
   (`engine/icvsp/consensus.py`).
The 1 s reuse is the only change in behaviour: in the system simulation one query in about 120 missed an advisory
for a hazard first reported less than a second earlier; warnings are unchanged.
